# Babyboel

Babyboel compares evidence-backed Dutch retail offers for diapers, diaper pants, and wipes while keeping product truth independent from retailer relationships and monetization.

## Language

**Listing**:
A retailer-, channel-, seller-, and retailer-SKU-specific presentation of one package.
_Avoid_: Product, offer

**Offer**:
The price, availability, purchase conditions, and effective period under which a listing can be bought.
_Avoid_: Listing, product

**Outbound destination**:
The verified retailer URL for the exact listing and seller represented by an offer.
_Avoid_: Affiliate URL, product URL

**Affiliate program**:
An approved commercial relationship under which an outbound visit or resulting purchase may earn Babyboel commission.
_Avoid_: Retailer integration, retailer activation

**Affiliate link**:
An outbound link carrying program-approved attribution for Babyboel without changing the offer, destination, inclusion, or ranking.
_Avoid_: Redirect, monetized offer
